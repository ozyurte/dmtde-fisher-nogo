## 📄 Abstract

We investigate Gaussian late-time dark energy transitions and establish that the linear growth amplitude σ₈ exhibits an intrinsic parametric collapse with R² = 0.9998. This leads to a **no-go theorem**: Δσ₈/σ₈ ≲ 0.03 for realistic transition widths.

**Key Results:**
- ✅ Rigorous no-go theorem for background-level dark energy modifications
- ✅ Fisher kernel decomposition reveals 200-fold information asymmetry
- ✅ Information bottleneck interpretation
- ✅ Implications for cosmological model selection
