## 📄 Abstract

We investigate Gaussian late-time dark energy transitions and establish that the linear growth amplitude σ₈ exhibits an intrinsic parametric collapse: σ₈(f_d0, z_c) → σ₈(f_d0) with correlation R² = 0.9998. Through systematic analysis using the CLASS Boltzmann code, we demonstrate this degeneracy leads to a **background-level no-go theorem**: Δσ₈/σ₈ ≲ 0.03 for realistic transition widths, insufficient to resolve the ~8% discrepancy in S₈ between Planck and weak lensing surveys.

**Key Results:**
- ✅ Rigorous no-go theorem for background-level dark energy modifications
- ✅ Fisher kernel decomposition reveals gradient cancellation as geometric origin
- ✅ Information bottleneck interpretation: observables act as low-pass filters
- ✅ Implications for model selection in cosmology
